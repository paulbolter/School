IELTS Academic · Writing Task 2

Write your outweigh essay

Choose your position, develop it clearly, and aim for at least 250 words.
Your Task
In many countries, fast food is becoming cheaper and more widely available.
Do the disadvantages of this development outweigh the advantages?
Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

What kind of question is this?

TypeOutweigh The question asks you to compare the advantages against the disadvantages and decide which side outweighs the other.

Decide if the advantages outweigh the disadvantages, or the other way around. Choose one.

How to structure it

  • Introduction: rephrase the topic and choose your side.
  • Body 1: fairly acknowledge the weaker side (the one you did NOT choose).
  • Body 2: develop your side in more depth — this is the side that "outweighs", so it gets more words.
  • Conclusion: refer very briefly to your body paragraph ideas to restate your side.
Your position: The disadvantages outweigh the advantages. Give the stronger side more explanation and support.
Introduction

Rephrase the topic

e.g. "Fast food is becoming cheaper and easier to find in many countries."

Then state your verdict

Make it clear the disadvantages are the stronger side.

Body 1 · Advantages (the weaker side)

Expand briefly on these ideas with further information or maybe an example.

Ideas you could use

  • Cheap meals help people on low incomes
  • Fast and convenient for busy workers and students
  • Creates jobs in restaurants and delivery
  • Reliable and available almost everywhere
Body 2 · Disadvantages (your side — the bigger paragraph)

Use only one or two of these ideas (not all of them), and expand on them with further information or an example.

Ideas you could use

  • High in fat, salt and sugar — linked to obesity and heart disease
  • Cheapness encourages people to eat it too often
  • Children develop unhealthy eating habits early
  • Traditional food culture and home cooking decline
  • Health problems cost societies money in the long run
Conclusion · Your final sentences to sum up your argument

Try to combine ideas from both body paragraphs into a complex sentence.

You may use “Although” or “Even though” to combine two opposing ideas.

Use the word outweigh. For example: the disadvantages clearly outweigh the advantages.
