IELTS Academic · Writing Task 1

The Map Task

Describe how a place changed over time · 150 words minimum · ~20 minutes
Your Task
The plans below show a harbour in 2000 and how it looks today.
Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
Write at least 150 words.

What kind of task is this?

TypeMap · change over time You are given the same place at two different times. Your job is to describe how it changed — what was added, removed, moved or replaced — not to give your opinion.

The maps compare past and present. Most of your verbs should be in the past simple passive: a hotel was built, the marina was relocated, the castle was demolished. See the Map Language tab for a full bank.
Don't forget what stayed the same. Strong answers also mention features that did not change. It shows the examiner you have read the whole map, not just the obvious differences.

What changed at Porth Harbour?

Changed

  • The disused castle was demolished; a hotel was built on the headland
  • The southern public beach became a private beach for hotel guests
  • The marina and the fishing boats swapped positions
  • Cafés & shops were added beside the lifeboat
  • A second car park and showers & toilets were added on the western side

Stayed the same

  • The dock in the north-east
  • The passenger ferries
  • The lifeboat station
  • The public beach in the north-east
  • The main road along the western edge

How to structure it

  • Introduction: rephrase the task — say what the two maps show and the two time points.
  • Overview: the most important paragraph. Give the big picture in 1–2 sentences — no detail yet.
  • Body 1: the tourism changes — the hotel, the private beach, the cafés & shops.
  • Body 2: the harbour re-organisation — the marina/fishing-boat swap, the new facilities, and what stayed the same.
The overview wins marks. Many students skip it and lose a whole band. After your introduction, step back and summarise the general change in one or two sentences before you go into detail.
Work down the four boxes in order. The prompts on the left are ideas and observations for that paragraph — use them, but write in your own words. Keep an eye on the counter: you need at least 150 words.

Model shape

"The two maps illustrate the changes that took place in Porth Harbour between 2000 and the present day."

Overview · The big picture (no detail)

The most important paragraph. One or two sentences — the general change only.

The big idea

  • The harbour became more focused on tourism and leisure
  • Some working features were kept, but several were moved or replaced

Model shape

"Overall, the harbour was redeveloped for tourism: a hotel and shops were added, while it still kept its role as a working harbour."

Avoid

Don't list every change here — save the detail for the two body paragraphs.

Body 1 · The tourism changes

Group the changes that made the harbour a tourist destination.

Ideas — pick and develop

  • The disused castle on the headland in the south-east was demolished and a hotel was built in its place
  • The public beach on the southern shore was converted into a private beach for hotel guests
  • Cafés & shops were added next to the lifeboat, in the centre

Useful verbs

was demolished · was replaced by · was built · was converted into

Body 2 · Re-organisation & what stayed

Group the moved/added facilities — then mention what did not change.

Ideas — pick and develop

  • The marina and the fishing boats swapped places — the fishing boats moved north, the marina moved south
  • A second car park and showers & toilets were added on the western side

Then — what stayed the same

  • The dock, passenger ferries and lifeboat remained unchanged
  • The northern public beach and the main road were kept

Useful verbs

was relocated · was moved · remained unchanged · was retained

A map task lives on two kinds of language: verbs of change (mostly in the past passive) and words for place and direction. Mix and match the options below.

Verbs of change — past simple passive

Addingwas built · was constructed · was added · was established · was set upA new hotel was built on the headland.
Removingwas demolished · was knocked down · was removed · was clearedThe disused castle was demolished.
Replacingwas replaced by · made way for · gave way toThe castle was replaced by a hotel.
Convertingwas converted into · was transformed into · was turned intoThe public beach was converted into a private beach.
Movingwas relocated · was moved · was repositionedThe marina was relocated to the south.
Keepingremained unchanged · was retained · was kept · stayed in the same placeThe lifeboat station remained unchanged.
Expandingwas extended · was enlarged · was expandedThe car parking area was extended.
Tense tip: because the maps compare the past with now, the verbs describing change are in the past. Use the passive when you don't say who did it — which on a map is almost always: a hotel was built, not they built a hotel.

Words for place & direction

Compassto the north / south / east / west of · in the north-east corner · along the southern shoreA hotel was built to the south-east of the harbour.
Next tonext to · beside · alongside · adjacent to · close toCafés and shops were added adjacent to the lifeboat.
Across fromopposite · facing · across fromThe marina now sits opposite the car park.
Edgeson the western edge · along the coast · on the headland · on the shoreThe main road runs along the western edge.
Centrein the centre · in the middle of the harbourThe lifeboat station stands in the centre.

Comparing then & now

Contrastwhereas · while · in contrast · by comparison · on the other handIn 2000 there was one car park, whereas today there are two.
Thenpreviously · formerly · originally · in 2000The site was originally occupied by a castle.
Nownow · currently · today · at presentThe headland is now home to a hotel.
