IELTS Academic · Writing Task 2

The Discussion Essay

Discuss both views and give your own opinion · 250 words minimum · ~40 minutes
Your Task
Many people feel that students should learn from online materials, while others feel that it is better to use printed materials.
Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

What kind of question is this?

TypeDiscussion This is a discussion essay. Your job is to fairly present what other people think about both sides — those who prefer online materials, and those who prefer printed ones.

This question asks for your opinion. The instruction says "give your own opinion," so you must include what you think — not just report the two views. Leaving your opinion out will cost you marks. (Some discussion questions only say "discuss both views" and do not ask for an opinion — but this one does.)

Two ways to answer

The same question can be answered with two different essay shapes. Both can score highly. Pick the one that suits how you think — then plan and write it in that tab.

  • Strategy 1 — Balanced. You stay neutral in the body: one paragraph for online materials, one for printed. You save your opinion for the conclusion.
  • Strategy 2 — Opinion-Led. You state your opinion in the introduction, argue mainly for the side you favour, then fairly acknowledge the other side before pushing back.
Notice the difference: in the balanced essay your opinion appears only at the end; in the opinion-led essay it appears at the start. Same question, opinion in a different place. Open each tab to see how the paragraphs change.
Balanced approach: Stay neutral in the two body paragraphs — present each view fairly without taking sides. Hold your own opinion back until the conclusion. The box sizes are a hint: the two body paragraphs (taller) carry most of your essay.
Introduction · Frame both views · no opinion yet
Rephrase the question in your own words. Introduce both views — that some prefer online materials and others prefer printed ones — and say you will discuss both. Don't give your opinion here.
Body 1 · The case for online materials
Explain why supporters prefer online materials — for example: instant access anywhere, easy searching, lower cost, video and interactive content, always up to date. You can cover more than one reason, but explain each one and add an example rather than just listing them. Stay neutral: this is their view, not yet yours.
Body 2 · The case for printed materials
Explain why supporters prefer printed materials — for example: easier on the eyes, better focus and memory, no device or internet needed, simple to highlight and annotate. Again, more than one reason is fine as long as each is developed with an explanation and an example. Stay neutral here too.
Conclusion · Your opinion goes HERE
Now give your own opinion. Which view do you find more convincing, and why? Summarise the two sides briefly and make your position clear. Don't add new ideas.

Opinion-led approach: Make your position clear from the very first paragraph. Argue mainly for the side you favour, then fairly acknowledge the other side and explain why it does not change your mind. The body boxes (taller) are where your argument lives.
Introduction · State your opinion NOW
Rephrase the question, then state your opinion clearly. Which type of material do you think is better — online or printed? Make your position obvious from the start.
Body 1 · Argue the side you favour
Develop the side you support. Give your reasons and a clear example. You can include more than one reason — just make sure each is explained and supported, not simply listed. This is your main argument, so make it your most detailed paragraph.
Body 2 · The other side — then push back
Fairly acknowledge the other view — what do its supporters argue? Then explain why it doesn't change your opinion (concede the point, then push back). This shows you've considered both sides.
Conclusion · Restate your opinion
Restate your opinion and briefly summarise your main reasons. Keep it consistent with your introduction. Don't introduce new points.
